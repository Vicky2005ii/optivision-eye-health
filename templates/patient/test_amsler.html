Amsler Grid Macular Map

Screening for Central Scotomas, Metamorphopsia & Macular Degeneration (AMD)

Eye:
Clinical Examination Instructions:
  • Hold your screen approximately 35 cm (14 inches) from your face (reading distance).
  • Wear your normal reading glasses if you usually wear them for close work.
  • Cover your other eye with your hand.
  • Fixate your gaze directly on the center white/red dot. While staring at the center dot, answer: • Do all grid lines look completely straight and unbroken? • Are there any dark holes, grey patches, blurry spots, or wavy distorted lines?
Marker Tool:
Tip: Click or drag on the grid to highlight any spots that look wavy, blurry, or missing.
