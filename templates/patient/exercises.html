20-20-20 Rule Coach & Ocular Relaxation

Relieve ciliary muscle spasm, replenish the tear film lipid layer, and prevent digital eye strain (asthenopia).

Clinical 20-20-20 Rule

Focus Relaxation Interval

Every 20 minutes of screen exposure, shift your gaze to look at an object at least 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds.

20:00 Screen Focus

Guided Ophthalmic Exercises

1. Full Blink Training

Screen users blink 66% less often. Close eyes gently for 2 seconds, pause, squeeze tight for 2 seconds, then release. Repeat 10 times to re-coat the cornea with tear lipids.

2. Near-to-Far Accommodation

Hold your thumb 10 inches from your nose. Focus on it for 5 seconds. Then shift focus to an object across the room (20+ feet away) for 5 seconds. Repeat 5 times to loosen ciliary spasm.

3. Warm Palming Technique

Rub palms together briskly until warm. Cup your palms gently over closed eyes without putting pressure on the eyeballs. Breathe deeply in total darkness for 60 seconds.
