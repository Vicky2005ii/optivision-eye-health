Interactive Eye Testing Center

Calibrated tests designed in accordance with ISO 8596 ophthalmic visual acuity and clinical retinal screening standards.

Testing Recommendations: Ensure your screen brightness is at ~80%, avoid glare from windows or lamps, wear your everyday distance correction (glasses/contacts) if testing aided acuity, and calibrate your screen size using a standard card for precise letter sizing.
Snellen / E-Chart

Visual Acuity Screening

Determines central vision clarity (from 20/200 down to 20/15). Supports monocular isolation (OD / OS), millimetric distance scaling (1m, 2m, 3m), and vocal response prompts.

Duration: 3 - 5 mins
Standard: 5 arcmin Snellen
Distance: 1 to 3 Meters
Start Acuity Test
14 Color Plates

Ishihara Color Deficiency

Clinical pseudoisochromatic plates to screen and classify congenital or acquired color vision deficiencies, including red-green (protan / deutan) and tritan defects.

Duration: 2 - 3 mins
Classification: Protan / Deutan
Distance: 50 - 60 cm (Arm length)
Start Color Test
Clock Dial & Fan

Astigmatism Dial Test

High-resolution 360-degree radial line pattern. Unequal sharp lines indicate refractive cylinder and principal meridian axis (e.g. 90° vs 180°).

Duration: 2 mins
Target: Corneal Asymmetry
Distance: 60 - 80 cm
Start Astigmatism Test
Pelli-Robson Scale

Contrast Sensitivity

Measures your ability to distinguish faint objects against low-contrast backgrounds. Serves as an early diagnostic marker for glaucoma and cataracts.

Duration: 3 mins
Range: 100% to 1.5% Contrast
Distance: 1 Meter
Start Contrast Test
Retina / AMD

Amsler Grid Macular Map

Evaluates central 10° of visual field. Features interactive digital canvas tool allowing patients to mark scotomas (blind spots) or wavy distorted lines (metamorphopsia).

Duration: 2 - 3 mins
Marker: Interactive Scotoma Pen
Distance: 35 cm (Reading dist.)
Start Amsler Test

Validated 12-item clinical OSDI survey measuring ocular surface symptoms, environmental triggers (wind, AC), and digital screen fatigue with automated severity grading.

Duration: 2 mins
Score: 0 - 100 Index
Categories: Normal, Mild, Mod, Severe
Start Dry Eye Survey
